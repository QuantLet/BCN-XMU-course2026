Name of QuantLet : The_Asymmetric_Spillover_Effects_of_Underlying_Asset_Volatility_on_NFT_Liquidity

Published in : Blockchain Technology and Cryptocurrencies (Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin)

Description : 'This project investigates the asymmetric spillover effects of Ethereum (ETH) underlying asset volatility on NFT market liquidity across market tiers. Using high-frequency 5-minute Binance candlestick data and a large-scale Kaggle NFT transaction dataset (1.15 GB, approx. 20 million records), the pipeline constructs a daily econometric panel covering the 2022 crypto market crash period (2022-01-01 to 2022-07-01). ETH Realized Volatility (RV), Bipower Variation (BPV), and Jump components are computed from intraday log-returns. NFT collections are stratified into Blue-chip (top 5% by cumulative historical volume) and Tail (bottom 50%) segments. The panel is analyzed using two complementary causal inference methods: (1) Vector Autoregression (VAR) with Cholesky-identified Orthogonalized Impulse Response Functions (IRFs), and (2) Jorda (2005) Local Projections (LP) with Newey-West HAC robust standard errors. Both methods consistently reveal a statistically significant, asymmetric response: Blue-chip NFT volume shows a sharp, transitory decline following a one-standard-deviation RV shock, while Tail NFT volume exhibits a prolonged, persistent depression, providing evidence of a flight-to-quality mechanism in digital asset markets.'
