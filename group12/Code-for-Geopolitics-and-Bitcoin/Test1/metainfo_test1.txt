Name of Quantlet: Oil Price-Global Liquidity Transmission Mechanism

Discription: This project investigates the transmission mechanism from oil prices to risk assets through global liquidity, using mediation analysis and structural break tests. The study examines how geopolitical conflicts (US-Iran conflict on March 2, 2026) amplify the oil-liquidity relationship. The analysis includes OLS regression, mediation effect testing (Sobel test), Chow break point test, interaction term modeling, rolling window regression, and comprehensive visualization.
